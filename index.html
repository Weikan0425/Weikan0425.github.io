Kan Wei

Doctoral Student in Signal and Information Processing · University of Chinese Academy of Sciences · Aerospace Information Research Institute, Chinese Academy of Sciences

I study multimodal AI for Earth observation. My work connects optical, SAR, hyperspectral, and LiDAR data with vision-language learning to improve how models perceive, understand, and reason about complex remote-sensing scenes.

I also build benchmarks for reliable evaluation across sensors and applications.
EmailGoogle ScholarGitHub

Research Focus

Focus 01

Multisensor fusion

Learning complementary representations from optical, SAR, hyperspectral, and LiDAR observations for segmentation, classification, and change detection.

Focus 02

Vision-language understanding

Adapting and evaluating vision-language models on non-RGB Earth observation data, with particular interest in benchmark design and grounded reasoning.

Focus 03

Open evaluation

Building reproducible benchmarks for multimodal change detection and high-resolution semantic segmentation.
